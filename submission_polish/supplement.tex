\section{Frozen causal gate and prospective extension}
The audited causal gate used only permitted source-time and causal-history features. An initial leakage failure was a false warning caused by scanning the YAML forbidden-pattern list itself. The corrected audit classified the gate as structurally valid with limitations. It selected B5 for 0\% of eligible comparisons, making its predictions identical to always using B3. The gate therefore failed as a prospective selector.

Gate scoring requires the thirteen instrumented source-time features, which the frozen object-level diagnostic table records for YOLO11n and YOLO11s only. Extension flow: YOLO11n had 45 detector-complete, 10 propagation-complete, and 9 gate-eligible logs; YOLO11s had 45 detector-complete, 45 propagation-complete, and 33 gate-eligible logs; RT-DETR-L had 21 propagation-complete logs and no gate-eligible logs.

The B3-versus-B5 contrast requires only predicted and target geometry and was therefore scored on the RT-DETR-L extension logs.

This statistic is defined in the image space where error is scored and is distinct from the ground-plane velocity comparison reported above.

\section{Runtime scope}
Median per-object propagation times were 0.2360~ms for YOLO11n B3, 0.2216~ms for YOLO11n B5, 0.2144~ms for YOLO11s B3, 0.2005~ms for YOLO11s B5, 0.3298~ms for RT-DETR-L B3, and 0.3094~ms for RT-DETR-L B5. In a separate 2,000-frame benchmark excluding detector inference and input/output, median total post-detection overhead was 1.26~ms per frame for B3 and 1.14~ms for B5; 95th percentiles were 2.51 and 2.14~ms. Association, velocity-estimation, and gate stages were not timed separately, so the small aggregate difference is not interpreted.
